Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

WiredToLaunch ("WTL", "we", "us") runs the 72-Hour Founder Sprint and related programs. This page explains what personal information we collect when you register, why we collect it, who we share it with, and what rights you have over it. We wrote it in plain language on purpose.

1. What we collect

When you register for the sprint, we collect:

WhatWhy
NameTo identify you and personalize messages
Email addressAccount and program communication
WhatsApp numberSprint nudges, group access, daily updates
Chronotype (early bird / day operator / night owl)To match you into a triad group with compatible schedules
Which page you registered fromInternal tracking of which channel brought you here
Browser/device informationBasic technical troubleshooting and analytics

If you choose the optional paid upgrade (Sprint Copilot), payment details are collected and processed entirely by our payment partner, Cosmofeed/SuperProfile, not by us directly. We never see or store your card or payment information.

2. How we use it

  • To register you for the sprint and match you into a triad
  • To send you WhatsApp updates related to the program (schedule reminders, your daily progress, leaderboard updates)
  • To understand which of our channels are working, in aggregate, not to track you individually beyond what's needed to run the program
  • To respond if you contact us with a question or request

We do not sell your personal information to anyone, for any reason.

3. Who we share it with

We use a small number of trusted service providers to run this program. Each only receives the information it needs to do its job:

  • Google Firebase/Firestore, our database provider, stores your registration record. Hosted on Google Cloud infrastructure in Mumbai, India.
  • AiSensy, our WhatsApp messaging provider, sends you program updates over WhatsApp on our behalf.
  • Cosmofeed/SuperProfile, our payment processor, handles payment only if you choose the optional paid upgrade. We do not receive or store your payment card details.

We do not share your information with advertisers or data brokers.

4. Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you have the right to:

  • Ask us what personal information we hold about you
  • Ask us to correct inaccurate information
  • Ask us to delete your information, subject to any legal or program-operation requirements that mean we need to retain some records
  • Withdraw consent to WhatsApp communications at any time by replying STOP to any message
  • Raise a grievance if you're unhappy with how we've handled your data

To exercise any of these rights, or to raise a concern:

Email hello@wiredtolaunch.in. We'll respond within a reasonable time.

5. How long we keep it

We keep your registration information for as long as reasonably needed to run the program you registered for, and for a limited period after in case you register again for a future cohort. If you ask us to delete it sooner, we will, except where we're required to keep a record for legal or accounting reasons.

6. Cookies and local storage

We don't use cookies or local storage for tracking, and no advertising trackers. If a registration fails to save due to a connection issue, a temporary copy of what you entered is kept in your browser's local storage on your own device only, so it isn't lost. It is not sent anywhere automatically and is not used for tracking.

7. Age requirement

This program is intended for founders and aspiring entrepreneurs aged 18 and over. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

8. Changes to this policy

If we materially change how we handle your information, we'll update this page and, where appropriate, let you know directly.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy, or about your data specifically, can be sent to hello@wiredtolaunch.in.
